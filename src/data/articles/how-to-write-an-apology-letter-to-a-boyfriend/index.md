A good apology to your boyfriend is shorter and more specific than you think. In [our study](/research/why-people-apologize) of 484 real apologies to a partner, the most common reasons were saying something hurtful (15.3%), not being there (13.6%), going silent (11.2%) and losing your temper (10.7%). The ones that work name the moment; the ones that don't say "sorry for everything."

## How to write it

1. **Lead with the apology.** "I'm sorry for what I said at dinner" in the first line, so he knows what this is about.
2. **Name the specific thing.** What you said, what you checked, what you missed — and when.
3. **Say what it did to him.** Made him feel distrusted, embarrassed, not a priority. Show you see it from his side.
4. **Take responsibility, without "but."** Context in one line at most. No turning it back on him.
5. **Offer one concrete change and some space.** Then let him answer in his own time.

## Sample letters

### For saying something hurtful

> Chris,
>
> I'm sorry for what I said at your brother's on Saturday. In front of everyone, I joked that you'd "never have a real job." It wasn't a joke, it was a dig, and I knew how much you've been worrying about work.
>
> You told me that in confidence and I used it to get a laugh. That's not okay.
>
> I'm proud of how hard you're trying, and I should have said that instead. Can we get breakfast tomorrow, just us?
>
> Lauren

### For jealousy and checking his phone

> Alex,
>
> I'm sorry I went through your phone on Wednesday while you were in the shower. When I saw a message from Jess, I accused you before I even asked.
>
> You've never given me a reason not to trust you, and I made you feel like a suspect in your own home.
>
> The insecurity is mine to work on, not yours to prove wrong. If something worries me again, I'll ask you directly instead of looking.
>
> Nina

### For breaking a promise

> Hey Marcus,
>
> I'm sorry I didn't come to your game on Friday. I promised I'd be there, then I stayed out with the girls and texted you at halftime.
>
> You'd asked me weeks ago and it mattered to you. I made it look like it didn't matter to me.
>
> Next home game, I'm there — and I'm bringing the sign.
>
> Bella

### Short version to text him

> Hey, I've been thinking about this morning. I'm sorry for snapping at you over the dishes — I was stressed about work and took it out on you. Can we talk tonight?

## What not to write

- **"I'm sorry if I upset you."** The "if" makes it sound like you're not sure you did.
- **"Sorry, but you did it first."** That's a fight, not an apology.
- **"I'm the worst girlfriend ever."** It turns the letter into him comforting you.
- **A long list of every mistake.** Focus on the one that hurt. The rest can wait.
- **"Answer me."** Asking to talk is fine; demanding a reply isn't.

## Before you send it

- Send it soon — a day or two after, once you're calm.
- Read it out loud. If it doesn't sound like you, change it.
- For anything big, follow up face to face.
- Then do the thing you said you'd do. That's what he'll remember.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I apologize to my boyfriend after hurting him?

Name exactly what you did and how it made him feel, take responsibility without excuses, and say one thing you'll do differently. Then give him time — don't push him to say it's fine.

### Should I write him a letter or just talk?

If talking keeps turning into arguing, a letter lets you say it all without being interrupted. For small things, a text and a hug are enough. For something that hurt him, write it, then talk.

### How long should a sorry letter to my boyfriend be?

About 100–250 words. Specific beats long.

### What if he doesn't forgive me right away?

That's normal. An apology isn't a trade for instant forgiveness. Keep your word on what you promised, and give him the space he asks for.
